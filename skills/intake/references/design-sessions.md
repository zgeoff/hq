# Design sessions the owner asks for

When the owner asks you for a design session, message `z-delivery` to launch it now. Give it:

- the issue, filed in Triage first if there is none;
- the source of the request, `chat:$ATC_SESSION_ID`.

`z-delivery` launches it even when the design sessions with the owner in them are at their limit in
[pacing](../../delivery-lead/references/pacing.md#design-session-limits). Those are the sessions on
issues with the Linear label `with-geoff`.

The owner's request counts as his approval. So offer him no choice of when the session starts or of
which session goes first.
