# investigation brief

The template for an Investigation issue's launch prompt. Its placeholders name parts of the delivery brief in `templates/brief.md`.

```text
Find out <question> for <GEO-n>, to inform <decision>.

<Paste the issue: Outcome, Context, Questions to answer, Acceptance.>

<The Permissions block from the delivery brief.>

Finish line: findings with evidence and a recommendation, written to <path> in <repo> in a ready PR, and a final report summarising them. List a follow-up only for one of the delivery brief's two reasons: another repo, or my decision. <The sentence on my name and invented text from the delivery brief.>

<The delivery brief's cloud imp line, on a cloud imp.>

<The scratch and cleanup paragraph from the delivery brief.>
```
